# Nutrimouse example data

These four CSV files are copied without modification from the `mvlearn==0.5.0`
wheel distributed on PyPI, at `mvlearn/datasets/nutrimouse/`. They are the data
loaded by `mvlearn.datasets.load_nutrimouse()` in the original Pyrea notebook.

- Source release: https://pypi.org/project/mvlearn/0.5.0/
- Source wheel: `mvlearn-0.5.0-py3-none-any.whl`
- Wheel SHA-256: `449a5c649176d4a61a0408844ad45908cfcf6825cc029aa5b876b7624a244df6`
- The mvlearn distribution's MIT license is preserved in [LICENSE.mvlearn](LICENSE.mvlearn).

The mvlearn loader cites the original Nutrimouse study and the CCA R package:

- Martin et al. (2007), *Novel aspects of PPARalpha-mediated regulation of lipid
  and xenobiotic metabolism revealed through a nutrigenomic study*, Hepatology.
  https://doi.org/10.1002/hep.21510
- Gonzalez et al. (2008), *CCA: An R Package to Extend Canonical Correlation
  Analysis*, Journal of Statistical Software 23(12).
  https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v023.i12

`gene.csv` has 40 rows and 120 features; `lipid.csv` has 40 rows and 21 features.
`genotype.csv` and `diet.csv` contain the corresponding two-class genotype and
five-class diet annotations, in the same row order. Row-based IDs generated by
the tutorial are convenience identifiers, not original biological identifiers.

The files are bundled so the graph tutorial runs without downloading data or
installing mvlearn and its comparison algorithms. Labels are for evaluation;
they are not supplied to graph execution or parameter selection.
